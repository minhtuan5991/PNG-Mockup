PNG Mockup invokes pngquant as a separate command-line program for its single
automatic PNG compression workflow. It uses up to 256 palette colors, quality
80-95, speed 4 and adaptive dithering. ICC/DPI/color chunks are restored from
the source and the palette PNG is then optimized losslessly with oxipng.
